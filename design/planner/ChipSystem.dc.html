System A — one channel per question
Seven things can be true of a card at once. Rather than seven colours, each visual channel answers exactly one question, so they never compete: the fill is about the placement, the edge is about where he came from, the glyphs are about the player.
Fill — this placement
Only one thing tints a card: he does not play this position. Everything else leaves the fill alone, so a tinted card always means the same thing.
Left edge — where he came from
A blue edge marks someone off a list rather than out of your squad. Scan the board and the incoming players stand apart without reading a word.
Glyphs — the player
Unwanted, shortlisted, injury-prone. These follow the man, so they look identical in every slot he occupies.
